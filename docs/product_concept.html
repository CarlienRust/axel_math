Full product concept for a South African primary school maths app

Core proposition

Mission
Free, offline-first maths for every SA child
Ages 5–13 (Gr R–7). Runs on the cheapest Android phone. No data required after first download.
Pedagogy
Concrete → pictorial → abstract
Matches how young children learn: physical objects first, then pictures, then symbols. Proven in Singapore and UK research.
Context
Built in South Africa, for SA
Taxi ranks, spaza shops, braais, imifino — familiar everyday settings. Characters with SA names and faces.
Language
11 official languages (phased)
Launch: English + isiZulu + isiXhosa + Afrikaans. Voice-over narration so non-readers can still access content.

Key features

  • Story-world gamification — kids build a SA village as they progress. Each topic unlocks a new building (spaza shop = multiplication, taxi rank = division).
  • Adaptive difficulty — the app detects gaps and loops back without the child feeling stuck or ashamed. Mastery-based, not time-based.
  • Teacher dashboard (lite) — a single shared class code lets a teacher see which skills 30+ kids are struggling with, even on a basic smartphone.
  • Parent/caregiver mode — simple weekly summary via WhatsApp (most accessible channel in SA). No app needed for parents.
  • True offline-first — full curriculum works with zero connection. Progress syncs automatically when WiFi or data is available.
  • Audio-first UI — every instruction can be listened to, not just read. Critical for early Grades R–2 where reading is still developing.

Recommended stack (lean & accessible)

Delivery format
Progressive Web App (PWA) — one codebase, installs from browser, no app store needed. Falls back to APK sideload for Android.
Frontend
React + Vite. Tiny bundle. Tailwind for styling. Framer Motion for child-friendly animations.
Offline engine
Service Workers + IndexedDB. All lesson content, audio, and images cached on first load (~15–20 MB total per grade).
Backend
Supabase (open source). Postgres for progress data. Row-level security per learner. Free tier covers early scale.
Audio/TTS
Pre-recorded MP3s for all 11 languages (AI voice + human QA). Bundled per grade to keep download size small.
Hosting
Vercel (free tier for PWA shell). Cloudflare CDN with SA edge nodes for fast first load. Zero-rate via MTN/Vodacom partnership.

Device targets

Minimum
Android 6+ / 1 GB RAM
Targets entry-level phones (R500–R800 range). 4" screen minimum. No camera or GPS needed.
Ideal
Android 10+ / 2 GB RAM
Smooth animations, richer interactions. Covers most government tablet distributions.
Also supported
Web browser (any)
Works at school computer labs, libraries. No install required — just a URL.

CAPS alignment by phase (Gr R–7)

Gr R–1
Foundation — concrete · Counting 1–20, number recognition, simple patterns, sorting by shape/colour, positional language. Audio-driven, tap-to-count interactions.
Gr 2–3
Foundation — bridging · Addition/subtraction to 100, multiplication as repeated addition, fractions (halves/quarters), length, mass, capacity using everyday SA objects.
Gr 4–5
Intermediate — pictorial · Whole numbers to 99 999, common fractions, basic decimals, properties of 2D/3D shapes, data handling with bar graphs.
Gr 6–7
Intermediate — abstract · Ratio and rate, percentages, integers, algebraic thinking, area and perimeter, probability, interpreting data.

CAPS content areas covered

Numbers, operations, relationships
Largest strand. 50–60% of curriculum time. Full coverage Gr R–7.
Patterns, functions & algebra
Sequence patterns, input/output tables, introductory algebra. Covered Gr 1–7.
Space & shape (geometry)
2D/3D shapes, symmetry, transformations. Visual-first — strong gamification potential.
Measurement
Time, length, mass, capacity, perimeter, area. Uses SA-contextual units and scenarios.
Data handling
Collecting, sorting, representing data. Introduced Gr 1, expands through Gr 7.

Sustainable free-access funding model

Phase 1 — grant funding · Apply to Sasol Foundation, Michael & Susan Dell Foundation, Allan Gray Orbis, and Telkom Foundation. All four fund EdTech in SA. Target R3–5M for build + first 2 years of operations.
Phase 2 — zero-rating deals · Negotiate with MTN, Vodacom, and Cell C (following Siyavula's model) to zero-rate the app's domain. This removes data cost as a barrier entirely, even for non-downloaded use.
Phase 3 — school/district licensing · Offer premium teacher dashboard + progress reporting + offline content packs to schools/districts at a low per-learner fee (R15–25/learner/year). Individual child access stays free forever.
Phase 4 — CSI partnerships · Corporate Social Investment from major SA corporates (FNB, Absa, Naspers/MultiChoice) who have public education commitments. In-kind: branding-free sponsorship of content production.
Long-term — DBE partnership · Work toward Department of Basic Education endorsement and distribution through the national SchoolNet SA channel. Creates scale and legitimacy without advertising.

What you must never do

  • No advertising to children — ever. It destroys trust and conflicts with the mission.
  • No selling learner data. Privacy must be POPIA-compliant from day one.
  • No hard paywalls on core content. Premium features are for schools, not children.
